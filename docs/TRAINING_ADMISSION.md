## Keep the targets separate

- Manipulation: requires independently documented conduct under a precisely defined
  protocol. Text style, repetition and incentives are observations, not proof of intent.
- AI authorship: requires separate provenance and a separate target; AI-written text
  can reflect real experience and human-written text can be deceptive. No such target
  is admitted here.
- Sentiment/aspect feedback: reports wording about experiences. It does not verify
  product performance, safety, authenticity or company quality. Existing rules remain
  experimental; a future learned sentiment task needs its own rights/evaluation scope.
- Unknown: missing provenance, unsupported proxy labels or reviewer disagreement
  remain unknown. Absence of suspicious wording is not a verified negative control.

The existing rt-labels-0.1 protocol requires two independent, blinded initial reviews
with supported evidence. Matching unsupported proxy votes do not establish truth;
unknown/disagreement blocks binary training rather than being forced into a class.
No label schema, rubric version or production methodology change is justified now.
